## Verified source defect and scoped correction

The buyer-visible checkout summary in `apps/customer_storefront/app/(english)/checkout/page.tsx` said **“Free delivery on orders over ৳500.”** Yet `app/api/checkout/route.ts` calculates `subtotal >= 500 ? 0 : 40`, and `DELIVERY_POLICY.freeDeliveryThresholdBdt` is 500. At exactly ৳500 the old wording is false. The checkout also did not repeat the published 1 km service boundary in that summary.

The proposed change derives both the inclusive threshold and radius from `DELIVERY_POLICY`: **“Free delivery on orders of ৳500 or more within 1 km of our Chawkbazar store.”** A static regression check guards this source copy. No fee calculation, API, order-creation logic, geofencing, schema, pricing, stock or production data was modified.

**Expected buyer benefit:** Accurate threshold and area information at the point of purchase; conversion effect is a hypothesis, not measured.

**Affected route:** `/checkout` (English component is also used for locale-aware checkout). Live checkout HTML was inaccessible to the public fetch tool; the source defect and policy were verified in code and live homepage policy.
