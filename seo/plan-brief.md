# SEO autopilot policy brief

This is the repository-owned execution brief distilled from the September 2026 SEO master plan and the evidence-controlled plans in `docs/GPT-SEO-Plan/`. It is a guardrail for unattended runs; it does not replace dated evidence or authorize production changes.

## Evidence gates

- Label every material claim `Verified live`, `Repo-confirmed`, `Third-party estimate`, `Manual observation`, `Assumption`, or `Needs verification`.
- A merged change, passing checks, or a source checkout does not prove production deployment, indexing, traffic, conversions, or ranking improvement.
- Use complete current and previous 28-day Search Console windows. Keep query exports separate from property totals, keyword estimates, and URL Inspection results.
- Do not claim an URL is indexed unless indexing evidence exists; otherwise say `not verified`.

## Safe prioritisation

- Prefer one evidence-backed conversion improvement per run.
- Page-2 opportunities require a real Search Console query/page row, a canonical indexable URL, intent match, and verified current facts.
- Product pilots should start with 10–20 stocked products and verified facts. Product content and matching structured data must be reviewed together.
- Keep the actual Chawkbazar 1 km service boundary truthful. Neighborhood pages, competitor alternatives, directory submissions, citation campaigns, and mass exact-match linking remain gated by evidence.

## Publishing gates

- Do not invent product attributes, brands, benefits, health claims, prices, stock, delivery terms, reviews, citations, or AI answers.
- Do not automatically deindex temporarily out-of-stock products or redirect discontinued products to unrelated pages.
- Keep Bengali pages to genuinely translated, indexable equivalents with reciprocal hreflang and self-canonicals.
- Every proposed source change needs an owner, evidence/source, exact URL, acceptance test, rollback method, baseline, observation window, and final result.

## Source precedence

The current repository state and fresh platform evidence take precedence over historical material in `docs/SEO Optimization/` and `docs/GPT-SEO-Plan/`. Those folders are research and history unless a dated source is independently re-verified.
