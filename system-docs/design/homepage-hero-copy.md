# Homepage hero copy

Audience: regular homepage visitors stocking their pantry and shopping for home essentials.
Goal: browse groceries and begin an order. Primary phrase: everyday groceries and home essentials.
Direction: familiar daily routines, with location reserved for delivery eligibility.

## English replacement

Eyebrow: Everyday / Lucky Store

Headline: A well-stocked home starts here.

Supporting copy: Rice for tonight. Tea for tomorrow. Shop groceries and home essentials from Lucky Store, delivered to your door. Check your order, then pay.

Mobile copy: Rice for tonight. Tea for tomorrow. Groceries and home essentials, delivered to your door.

Primary CTA: Shop groceries → /category
Secondary link: Check delivery area → /delivery

Delivery reassurance: use the shared delivery policy values for coverage and free-delivery threshold; retain payment after inspection.

Product rail heading: A few essentials to get you started

## Editorial review

Lead with a well-stocked home and familiar meal and tea routines. Keep concrete grocery examples, policy-backed delivery reassurance, and links to shopping and delivery information. Keep location in the eligibility line rather than the headline. Replace “Quick picks from today” with a neutral introduction because the rail does not establish a daily editorial selection.

Existing homepage metadata and structured data remain suitable for this hero rewrite. No additional discount, speed, availability, review, or popularity claims are introduced.
