# Product marketing guidance

Use this document for product-marketing, campaign, social, brand-voice,
audience, positioning, website-messaging, and promotional-copy work.

## Evidence boundaries

- Treat marketing angles, audience descriptions, objections, goals, and sample
  copy as hypotheses or drafts unless a current source supports them.
- Verify changeable prices, product availability, delivery terms, hours, and
  promotions against the current storefront or the owner before publishing.
- Do not invent testimonials, customer quotes, order counts, partnerships,
  product claims, discounts, delivery coverage, or performance guarantees.
- Keep payment and delivery wording aligned with the storefront policy. Do not
  imply advance payment is required when the approved flow supports payment at
  the doorstep after inspection.

## Lucky Store reference

The customer storefront is the source of truth for current brand presentation,
product information, delivery policy, and customer-facing claims. When sources
conflict, flag the conflict and verify it before publishing. Keep copy warm,
neighborly, practical, and concise; use Bangla-first social copy when the
channel and audience call for it.
