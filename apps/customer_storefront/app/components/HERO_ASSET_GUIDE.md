# Lucky Store 1947 campaign asset guide

Use these constraints for every replacement hero asset so the carousel stays crisp,
believable, and visually consistent.

## Production frame

- Canvas: 2400 × 1350 pixels (16:9), exported as AVIF and WebP at 400, 600, 800,
  and 1200 pixels wide.
- Background: transparent or Deep Night `#0B0B0D`.
- Safe area: keep the principal product group inside the center 50–60% of the
  frame, leaving at least 20% clear edge space across the left and right sides.
- Lighting: one warm saffron `#f0c444` rim light, soft neutral key light, controlled
  shadows, no multicolor neon.
- Product truth: use real Lucky Store packaging references. Preserve label text,
  proportions, seals, and brand colors exactly.
- Composition: one dominant product story with two or three supporting objects;
  no crowded grocery collages.

## Prompt structure

> Premium editorial product photograph for Lucky Store 1947, [real products and
> arrangement], authentic Chittagong neighborhood-grocer atmosphere, Deep Night
> #0B0B0D panoramic studio, restrained saffron #f0c444 rim light, realistic
> materials and packaging, subtle depth, high-energy asymmetric composition,
> center 55% subject safe area, 22% transparent edge clearance on both sides,
> crisp commercial photography, wide 16:9, no text overlay.

## Negative prompt

> invented logos, misspelled packaging, fake label text, floating groceries,
> excessive neon, crypto symbols, holographic coins, glossy plastic food,
> impossible reflections, duplicate products, crowded collage, coupon text,
> embedded typography, watermark, generic supermarket aisle, oversaturated color.

## Review gate

Before shipping, compare the asset with the catalog entry and reject any image
that invents a product, discount, voucher, delivery promise, or packaging detail.
